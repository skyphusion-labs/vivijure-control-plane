### feat(credits): a zero-balance floor on the proxied RunPod submit when CREDITS_ENFORCING is on (fleet-chezmoi#2049)

With `CREDITS_ENFORCING` on, `handleProxySubmit` calls `decideSubmit` with `required_micro_usd = SUBMIT_FLOOR_MICRO_USD` (one micro-USD), so a tenant whose available credit is at or below zero is refused with `402 insufficient_credit` and the plane-refusal header before anything reaches RunPod. An unreadable balance, or enforcement with no credit store, refuses with `503 balance-unknown` (fail closed). With the knob off nothing changes. This is a floor, not a price or a ceiling: any positive balance is admitted, so about one job past zero per tenant still runs (more with concurrent submits), and no hold is taken. The per-job price stays the open decision on cp#190. `SUBMIT_GATE_WIRED` is now `true`, and the credit views report `enforcing: true` with an `enforcement_reason` naming the floor and that residual exposure.
