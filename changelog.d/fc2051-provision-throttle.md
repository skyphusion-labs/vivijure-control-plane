### fix(provision): throttle provisioning per account and per client address (fleet-chezmoi#2051)

`POST /api/tenant/provision` now goes through `CP_RATE_LIMIT`, as `env.ts` already said it did. The limit applies twice, once keyed on the account and once on a SHA-256 of `cf-connecting-ip` (never the raw address), after slug validation and before any lookup or cloud write. A refusal is `429 rate_limited` with `retry-after: 60`, and nothing is created. A plane whose provisioner is configured but whose `CP_RATE_LIMIT` binding is missing answers `503 rate_limit_unconfigured` instead of provisioning unthrottled. The binding's own window applies (5 per 60 s in `wrangler.toml.example`); a longer-window cap such as studios per account per day is not part of this change. `docs/front-door-assets.md` drops the retired `runpod_api_key` field from the provision row.
