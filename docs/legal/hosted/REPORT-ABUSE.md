# Reporting abuse on the hosted Vivijure studio

> **Status: DRAFT, not in force.** This page describes the hosted studio, which has not opened to
> signups yet. It takes effect the day it does.

**If a child is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number first. Do not wait for us.**

---

## Where to report

**`abuse@skyphusion.org`**

That address reaches a human. You do not need an account with us to use it, and you do not need to
be the person affected.

For suspected child sexual abuse material, **please also report directly to NCMEC's CyberTipline at
[report.cybertip.org](https://report.cybertip.org) or 1-800-843-5678**, and to law enforcement. Do
not wait for us to relay it. If you are outside the United States, your national hotline is listed
at [inhope.org](https://www.inhope.org). Reporting to them and to us at the same time is the right
move; there is no reason to choose.

Other addresses, if your issue is not abuse:

| Address | For |
|---|---|
| `abuse@skyphusion.org` | Abuse, illegal content, harassment, impersonation, anything on this page |
| `privacy@skyphusion.org` | Privacy questions, data requests about yourself |
| `legal@skyphusion.org` | Policy questions, copyright notices, legal process |

## Why your report matters more here than on most platforms

**We do not monitor what our users create.** We run no content scanning, no classifiers, and no
automated review of prompts or generated output. That is a deliberate privacy commitment, not an
oversight, and we are not going to quietly change it.

The direct consequence: **a report from a person is our primary way of learning that something is
wrong.** We are not watching, so when you tell us, you are usually telling us something we did not
know. We treat it that way.

## What to include

You do not need all of this. Send what you have. An incomplete report is far better than no report.

**The most useful things:**

1. **A link or address** -- the studio URL (something like `name.studio.vivijure.com`), or a link to
   the specific content.
2. **What you believe is wrong with it**, in your own words. You do not need to cite a policy
   section or use legal language.
3. **When you saw it** (a date and rough time, with your time zone).
4. **How you came across it**, if you are comfortable saying. It helps us judge how the material is
   being distributed.
5. **A contact address**, if you want acknowledgement or follow-up questions.

**Please do NOT attach suspected child sexual abuse material to your report.** Describe it and tell
us where it is. Sending it forwards it, which we do not want you to do and which creates legal
problems for you and for us. A link and a description are enough.

---

## What we do when a report arrives

1. **We log it**, with the time it arrived.
2. **We triage it.** Suspected child sexual abuse material and any imminent risk of physical harm
   jump every other queue, at any hour.
3. **We suspend first when the report is serious.** Suspension takes the studio offline immediately
   and is reversible. It destroys nothing, which matters, because evidence has to survive.
4. **We look only as far as we have to.** We check the specific thing you identified, and nothing
   else. We do not search the account, we do not read through unrelated projects, and we stop the
   moment we know what we are dealing with. We are not investigators and we do not act like them.
5. **For child sexual abuse material we report to NCMEC's CyberTipline and cooperate with law
   enforcement.** In the United States this is a legal obligation, not a favor we choose to do.
6. **We preserve the evidence** for as long as the law requires, and we do not delete it to be
   helpful to anyone.
7. **We terminate** accounts that violate the bright-line rules, permanently.

We will not tell you what we found, what we did about it, or whether an account exists. That is not
evasiveness; an abuse channel that reports back on other people's accounts is a tool for harassment.
If you gave us a contact address we will acknowledge that we received your report.

## The rules we enforce

The full policy is the [hosted Acceptable Use Policy](aup/1.0.0.md). In short:

**Absolutely prohibited, no exceptions, no context in which it is acceptable:**

- **Child sexual abuse material, including material that is synthetic or AI-generated.** There is no
  "it is not a real child" exception here. This is the one place where our otherwise strong privacy
  stance yields, and it yields completely.
- **Non-consensual intimate imagery**, including synthetic imagery of real people.
- **Non-consensual sexual deepfakes of real people.**

**Also prohibited:** targeted harassment, impersonation for deception, infringing use of other
people's work, and attacks on the platform or on other tenants.

## What we can and cannot reach

We would rather be plain about this than have you send a report to the wrong place:

| Situation | Can we act? |
|---|---|
| A studio we host at `*.studio.vivijure.com` | **Yes.** We can suspend and terminate it. |
| Someone running Vivijure on their own hardware | **No.** Vivijure is free open-source software. A self-hosted instance never talks to us, we cannot see it, and we cannot switch it off. Report it to whoever hosts it, or to law enforcement. |
| GPU rendering on a user's own RunPod account | **Partly.** We can shut down the studio we host. The GPU endpoints are on their RunPod account under their contract with RunPod, and we cannot touch them. Law enforcement and RunPod can. |

This is a real limit of an open-source, bring-your-own-compute product, and pretending otherwise
would waste your time at the moment you are trying to get something stopped.

## If you are the person in the content

Contact `abuse@skyphusion.org` and say so. Reports about non-consensual intimate imagery or
sexual deepfakes of yourself go to the front of the queue alongside the other serious categories,
and we do not require you to prove your identity before we act on a credible report.

You do not have to describe the material in detail. A link and "this is me and I did not consent"
is enough to start.

## Copyright

Copyright complaints go to `legal@skyphusion.org` with the URL, identification of the work, and your
contact details.

## Retaliation and good faith

We do not share reporter identities with the accounts reported on. We do not act on reports as a way
of settling scores between users, and a pattern of bad-faith reports is itself an acceptable-use
problem.
